# SellYou - Freelance Services Marketplace

A platform connecting business owners with talented freelance creatives.

## Vision

SellYou is an open-ended freelance marketplace where:
- **Business Owners** can search and discover freelancers by the type of work they need (design, writing, video, music, photography, development, etc.)
- **Freelancers** can create professional profiles showcasing their services, portfolio, rates, and expertise

## How It Works

### For Business Owners
1. Search by service type (e.g., "graphic design", "copywriting", "web development")
2. Browse freelancer profiles with portfolios and reviews
3. Contact freelancers and hire them
4. Manage projects and payments

### For Freelancers
1. Create a profile showcasing their skills and services
2. Upload portfolio work and examples
3. Set their rates and availability
4. Receive and manage job inquiries
5. Build reputation through client reviews
